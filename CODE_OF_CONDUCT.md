# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Expected: empathy and kindness; respect for differing opinions; giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback; accepting responsibility for
mistakes; focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language, imagery or advances; trolling, insulting
comments, personal or political attacks; public or private harassment;
publishing others' private information; other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this Code.
It applies in all community spaces and when representing the community in
public. Report violations to **noe.fontana.pro@gmail.com** (one person reading
one mailbox; expect an acknowledgement within a week). If a report involves that
person, use GitHub's abuse-reporting form. Reports are handled promptly and
privately. Consequences: **Correction**, **Warning**, **Temporary Ban**,
**Permanent Ban**.
