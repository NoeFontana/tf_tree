## [0.0.4] — 2026-08-22 (the slot a killed participant keeps)

### Added
- `ParticipantTable::reclaim` in `tf_tree_core`: frees a dead participant's slot, guarded by the observed state word; also accepts `RESERVED` (0028 step 1).
- `Tree::reap_participants()`: reclaims records of processes whose OFD lock byte is released, returns the count; returns `0` on a read-only tree (0028 step 5).

### Changed — breaking
- `Tree::attach_shared` and `attach_shared_at` refuse `AttachMode::ReadWrite` with `ShmError::ReadWriteNeedsRendezvous`; writers must join through `tf_tree::Open`, and `ReadOnly` is unchanged.

### Fixed
- A `SIGKILL`ed read-write participant kept its slot forever; the slot assigner and hangup callback now decide liveness from the OFD lock byte and reclaim dead records, including `RESERVED` (#184; 0028 steps 3-4).
- A participant lock byte and arena record could carry different indices; `Open` now refuses with the new `OpenError::ParticipantSlotDiverged` (#201; 0028 step 0c).
- `Open::await_open` returned immediately for any whole-second budget; the per-attempt timeout is now truncated to whole microseconds.

### Known issues
- A `fork`ed child keeps its parent's slot alive; no collector touches it (0030; `TFT014`).
- An arena whose owner died cannot be rejoined; `OpenError::TakeoverUnsupported` is returned instead (`docs/RUNBOOK.md`).
- `Tree::reap_participants` frees the record of a live `build_shared` creator served by hand through `OwnerServer`; serving such an arena is out of contract (0031).
- `Tree::reparent` decides topology-lock liveness from `/proc` (#213; 0029).
